About

Media with
purpose & precision.

Ahmad Jamal Terro is a Digital Media Consultant & Producer based in Lebanon. A graduate of Beirut Arab University in Mass Communication, Ahmad started his career in journalism and news content production before moving into social media and digital strategy — bringing together strategic thinking and hands-on production, from shaping a brand's voice to crafting the content that delivers it.

With 6 years of experience across journalism and social media, Ahmad has led content strategy and production for clients across cultural, wellness, medical, and professional sectors. Together with his team, he directs and covers events, and runs Meta Ads campaigns — from targeting the right audience to testing creative and managing budgets. He previously led social media at Al Warsheh Studios, overseeing the brand's media production.
